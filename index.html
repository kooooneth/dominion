How to Play

  • It's real time. There is no end-turn — gold, food and armies all flow continuously. Use the ⏸ / 1× / 2× / 3× speed controls any time.
  • Navigate the world: drag to pan, scroll or use +/− to zoom. The world is large, with continents and ~30 islands — some holding special sites (💎 gold, 🛢️ oil, 🌱 delta, 🏛️ ruins, 🗿 fortress) that reward whoever holds them.
  • Select a province you own → set the deploy slider → click an adjacent tile (or a coast in range, if it has a ⚓ Harbor) to send an army. Armies march and fight where they land — you'll see it happen.
  • Build (up to 5 per province) with gold: Factory/Mine/Market/Bank (gold), Farm (food), Barracks (army cap), Fort/Watchtower (defense), Hospital (heal), Harbor (cross seas), Lab (+income), Silo (missiles). Every building shows on the map.
  • 🚀 Missiles (need a Silo): click the rocket button, then any enemy tile except a ★ capital. It devastates the target and the tiles around it, then reloads on a cooldown. Defend with 🛰 Air Defense (a chance to shoot incoming missiles down) and a 🔰 Shield (blocks one hit — missile or army — then it's spent). Capitals can only be taken by a marching army.
  • Save your deploy size: hit 🔓 save next to the slider and the chosen troop count is kept for every province, so you don't reset it each time.
  • Rival bots grow, build, sail with harbors and attack you — invade their land and they'll fight back. Difficulty sets how rich, fast and vicious they are.
  • Modes: 🌍 Conquest (hold half the world), ⚡ Blitz (fast & rich), 👑 Regicide (take every rival capital), 🛡 Last Stand (outlast a bigger, escalating world). Difficulty runs from Peaceful to Impossible.
  • Living world: provinces slowly raise a local militia, armies march slower through mountains & jungle, and random events strike — gold rushes 💰, plagues ☠, rebel uprisings ⚔, harvests 🌾 and migrations 👥. Grab special sites (💎🛢🌱🏛🗿⛰⛪) for lasting bonuses.
  • Win/Lose: hit the mode's goal to win; you always lose if your capital falls.
